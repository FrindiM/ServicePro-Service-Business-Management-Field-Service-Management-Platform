# Customer Portal Guide

The customer portal is separate from the staff login and is scoped to one tenant and one customer record.

Customers can:

- view their profile, sites and assets
- create a Service Request
- follow request/job progress
- view Quotations and approve them
- view Invoices and balances
- upload payment proof for Finance review
- download quotation/invoice/service-report PDFs
- view active Contracts and Warranties
- view service history relevant to their account

Uploading payment proof does **not** automatically mark an invoice paid. Finance must approve the proof; only then is a Payment posted and invoice balance recalculated.
