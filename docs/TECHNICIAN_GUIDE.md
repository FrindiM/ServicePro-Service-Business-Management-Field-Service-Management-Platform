# Technician Guide

Technician accounts receive a mobile-oriented job workflow and only see jobs permitted by their role/assignment.

## My Jobs

Open **Jobs** to see assigned/current work. A Job detail contains customer/site/asset information, schedule, notes, required materials and collaboration history.

## Field workflow

1. **START TRIP** changes the job to On The Way.
2. **CHECK IN** asks the browser for GPS. If the customer site has coordinates, ServicePro calculates the distance from the site. GPS failure does not block work; it records a warning instead.
3. **START WORK** begins work.
4. Add one or more **Work Logs** with problem, action, result and recommendations.
5. Upload **Before / During / After** photos.
6. Record **Material Usage** from available warehouse stock. The system blocks quantities greater than available stock.
7. Record field expenses and receipt attachments when needed.
8. **PAUSE** sets On Hold when work is interrupted.
9. **COMPLETE** finishes the work.
10. Capture technician/customer signatures and optional customer rating/feedback.

The completed Job can generate a branded Service Report containing work logs, materials and signatures.
